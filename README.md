# 🍦 Brain Freeze NYC

**Made by:** Katie Hariprashad

## 🚚 About

You run an ice cream truck around NYC. Customers come up with orders, and you have to make them right before they get impatient and leave. You get 5 days in 5 different neighborhoods 🗽, and it gets harder every day. After each day you get a dashboard with your stats 📊 (revenue, accuracy, wait time, bounce rate) plus reviews from your customers.

## ▶️ How to start

Play it here: https://katiehariprashad.github.io/brain-freeze-nyc/

Or download `index.html` and open it in Chrome. No setup or packages needed.

It works best on a laptop 💻. Turn your sound on because there's music 🎶.

## 🎮 Rules

1. 🧾 A customer walks up and their order shows at the top: cup or cone, scoops, then toppings.
2. 🍦 Pick the right cup or cone.
3. ✋ Hold down a flavor tub and let go when the meter is in the green zone. The first scoop goes on the bottom.
4. 🍒 Add the toppings.
5. 🛎️ Hit Serve before their patience bar runs out. If it runs out, they walk away 💨.

👉 The first customer shows you step by step what to do. After that you're on your own.

⭐ Each order is scored out of 100 based on the cup or cone, flavors, scoop size, toppings and speed. Better scores get you more stars and bigger tips 💵. Each day the meter gets faster ⚡, the green zone gets smaller and customers get less patient.

## 🖱️ Controls

Mouse only.

- 🍦 Click a cup or cone to pick it
- ✋ Hold and release a flavor tub to scoop
- 🍒 Click a topping to add it, click again to remove it
- 🛎️ **Serve** sends the order
- ↩️ **Undo** removes your last scoop
- 🗑️ **Start over** clears the order
- ⏸️ **Pause** pauses the game
- 🎵 turns music on/off, 🔊 turns sound effects on/off
